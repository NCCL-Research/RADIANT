# Annotation protocol template

**Status:** Blank template. Completing this document does not, by itself,
establish that a taxonomy is valid or that annotations are reliable.

Use this template to specify reference-label procedures before model outputs
are evaluated. Keep the protocol versioned alongside the benchmark release.

## 2. Label set

For each label, document:

| Field | Required specification |
|---|---|
| Label ID | Stable machine-readable identifier |
| Display name | Short human-readable name |
| Operational definition | Observable criteria that must be met |
| Inclusion evidence | Evidence sufficient to assign the label |
| Exclusion evidence | Evidence that rules the label out |
| Boundary cases | Cases that distinguish this label from related labels |
| Co-occurring labels | Whether multiple labels may be assigned and under what rule |

Do not infer a misconception solely from an incorrect final answer. Identify
the specific intermediate evidence required by the operational definition.

## 3. Ambiguous and insufficient evidence

Specify separate handling rules for:

- Ambiguous or contradictory work.
- Work compatible with more than one label.
- Correct answers with insufficient visible reasoning.
- Incorrect answers with no interpretable working.
- Blank, incomplete, or off-task responses.
- Cases outside the defined taxonomy.

Define when annotators must mark a case as insufficient evidence rather than
guessing a diagnosis. Specify whether multi-label annotation is permitted.

## 5. Reliability and adjudication reporting

Pre-specify:

- The agreement statistic(s) and why they fit the label structure.
- Whether agreement is calculated before adjudication.
- How missing, multi-label, and insufficient-evidence cases are handled.
- The number and proportion of cases requiring adjudication.
- How disagreements and protocol changes are documented.

Do not report a single agreement statistic without its unit, denominator,
label structure, and relevant assumptions.

## 6. Evidence traceability

For each assigned label, record:

- The exact span or step supporting the decision, where available.
- The rule in the operational definition that the evidence satisfies.
- Any competing interpretation considered.
- Whether the evidence is direct, ambiguous, or absent.

Keep evidence-span quality separate from label correctness.
